Privacy Policy — Planet Tvale

App: Planet Tvale (com.telo.spacecounter) · Developer: XTelo · Last updated: 30 June 2026

Planet Tvale is an educational game for learning the Georgian alphabet (ქართული ანბანი) and numbers. We respect your privacy and have designed the app to collect as little data as possible. This policy explains what is and is not collected.

1. Information we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number, location, or contacts.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not show advertisements.
  • We do not use the device Advertising ID.
  • We do not sell or share any personal data with third parties.

2. Information stored on your device

Game progress and settings (such as which letters you have unlocked) are saved locally on your device only. This data never leaves the device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

3. Purchases & ads

This version of Planet Tvale is completely free. It contains no in-app purchases and no advertisements, and uses no payment or advertising SDK.

4. Children's privacy

Planet Tvale is suitable for all ages and contains no content unsuitable for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not contain ads, social features, chat, or user-generated content. We comply with applicable children's privacy laws including the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, including GDPR-K).

5. Data retention & deletion

Because game data is stored only on your device, you can delete all of it at any time by uninstalling the app. To request deletion of any purchase-related record held by our provider, contact us at the email below.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email giorgi.teliashvili@gmail.com.

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
